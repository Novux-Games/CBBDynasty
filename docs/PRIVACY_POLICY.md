# Privacy policy — Dynasty: College Basketball

**Effective date:** 2026-11-10  
**Publisher:** Novux Games ([novux.dev](https://novux.dev))  
**Play Console URL:** `PlayStoreListingCopy.PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` (`https://novux.dev/privacy/dynasty-college-basketball` — host this document or an equivalent page before store submission)

## Summary

Dynasty: College Basketball is **offline-first**. We do not operate accounts, analytics, or ad networks in the shipping app. Dynasty progress is stored **locally on your device**.

## Data we collect

| Data | Collected? | Notes |
|------|------------|--------|
| Account / email | **No** | No sign-in |
| Dynasty saves | **Local only** | SQLite on device |
| App settings | **Local only** | DataStore preferences |
| Crash / analytics | **No** | Not integrated in current build |
| Contacts / location | **No** | Not used |

## Network use (optional)

The app may contact the internet **only when you choose**:

- **Import league identity pack from URL** — one-shot HTTPS fetch from allowlisted hosts ([`PackImportConstants`](../android/core/src/main/kotlin/dev/novux/dynasty/cbb/core/pack/import/PackImportConstants.kt))

We do not upload dynasty saves to Novux servers as part of normal play.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

- Delete saves from the in-app **Load** screen.
- Uninstall the app to remove local data.
- Skip URL pack import to avoid network use entirely (embedded league ships with the app).

## Changes

We may update this policy before store releases. Material changes will be noted in app release notes and by updating the effective date above.
