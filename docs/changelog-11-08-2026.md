# November 8, 2026

Dynasty: College Basketball can open on Android, and the shape of a full season — offseason through March — is now locked in.

## In the app

Cold start is a short **Novux Games** card (tap to skip), then the game splash, then **Tap to Play**. Home is a simple menu:

- **Continue** appears once a save exists
- **New**
- **Load**
- **Settings**
- **Credits**

The title on that screen is **Dynasty: College Basketball**. The menu is on screen; the buttons do not create or load a dynasty yet. Saves, the new-game wizard, and the logo on the splash are next.

## Starting a career

A new dynasty is six steps: coach, archetype, offense and defense, house rules, school, contract. You start in the offseason, not on opening night. The coaching carousel shows up from season two on. Year one, you just take the job.

Job security is on your coach card. The school staff page splits assistants from support staff.

## The year

- **Offseason, nine weeks.** Who stays, four weeks of recruiting and the portal, summer work, roster cuts, fundraising, then conference offers.
- **One preseason week.** Setup tasks, including tournament invites. Finish the list and the season can start.
- **Up to three exhibition weeks.** Those games stay off your record.
- **Regular season, 32 games.**
- **Conference tournaments.**
- **National postseason, seven weeks** for every program.
- **End of Season.** Final rankings, grades, and prestige. No games that week.

Early-season events sit inside the regular season: the Island Classic, the Desert Shootout, the Caribbean Showdown, and the Blue Blood Tip-Off. A team enters at most one. If you lose before the event ends, the leftover weeks are byes. You do not get a fill-in game.

The last result on the dashboard opens that game’s report.

## Rankings

All **365** teams are ranked. The Top 25 is the highlighted cut of that list.

- **NCBA NET** is the résumé order. Selection Sunday uses it. Program prestige stays out of the formula.
- **Media Poll** and **Coaches Poll** are the human polls. They can lean on prestige, streaks, and storylines. Poll colors belong to these two lists.
- **Schedule strength** is a 0–99 rating of the slate you played.

## March

**124** teams get a national postseason. **241** go home after their conference tournament, or after the regular season if they missed that bracket.

| Event | Field |
| --- | --- |
| NCBA Men's Basketball Tournament | 76 |
| NCI (National College Invitational) | 32 |
| National Heritage Classic | 16 |

The NCBA field is 32 conference champions plus 44 at-large teams. The top 20 at-large teams go straight to the main bracket. The next 24 play an Opening Round, and 12 advance. The bracket from there is 64 teams. NCI and the Heritage Classic run in the same window.

Seeds always name the tournament: `NCBA #4`, `NCI #1`, `HC #12`. The hub for the month is **March Mayhem**. NCBA rounds: Opening Round, Round 1, Round 2, Super 16, Great 8, Championship 4, National Championship.

Programs moving up from a lower division can win their conference tournament and can be selected for the Heritage Classic. They are out of the NCBA and the NCI until the transition is finished (year 4). If one of them wins a conference tournament, they keep the title and the NCBA automatic bid passes to the next eligible team, starting with the runner-up.

## The league

The default world is the **2026–27** season: **365** programs, **32** conferences. 361 are fully certified. Four are in a reclassification year. Every school has a home city and a home state, and recruiting uses the state.

Real school and conference names stay in a reference list. The game uses its own names on screen.

## Recruiting

The high school and JUCO class is created in the preseason, so a **Top 60** is in the news all season. You can browse it. Offers wait.

Offers, NIL, and commits run for four weeks in the offseason (weeks 2–5), next to the transfer portal. Scouting reads as letter grades. There are no campus visits. Half of your unused recruiting points carry into the next week.

## On the court

Games are simulated one possession at a time, with a **30-second** shot clock. Playback can run slower or faster. That changes how quickly you read the game, and leaves the clock inside the sim alone.
